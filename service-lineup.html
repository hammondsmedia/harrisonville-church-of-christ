  8. 8 Communion Lord's Supper
    About Communion The Lord's Supper is observed only at the Lord's Day morning service (Acts 20:7), imitating the example Jesus gave the night He was betrayed (1 Corinthians 11:23). The unleavened bread and the cup (containing unleavened grape juice) are passed to each disciple, each giving thanks. Communion portrays our unity in Christ (1 Corinthians 10:17) and calls each communicant to examine themselves (1 Corinthians 11:28). As a visitor, you will not be expected to participate.
  9. 9 Contribution Giving
    About the Contribution The collection is taken only at the Lord's Day morning service (1 Corinthians 16:1-2). Each disciple gives a portion of money proportionate to their income (2 Corinthians 8:12). As a visitor, you will not be expected to contribute.
  10. 10 Sermon Teaching
    About the Sermon The teaching service is conducted by men speaking one at a time (1 Corinthians 14:26-35). The teaching is drawn from the Bible and designed to instruct and encourage attitudes and behavior that please God. You are invited to follow along in your Bible and take notes. Teachers and congregation leaders will be available to answer questions after services are dismissed.
